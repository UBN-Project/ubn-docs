## Why email verification exists

Before you can upload your company documents or access any part of the platform, you need to prove that you have access to the email address you registered with. This matters for two reasons.

First, it prevents anyone from accidentally registering with someone else's email address. Second, your email is how we send you security alerts, document review results, API key notifications, and production approval updates. Verifying it now means those messages reach the right person.

---

## What to expect in the verification email

Within seconds of completing registration, an email lands in the inbox you provided. Here's what to look for:

| Email detail | Value |
|---|---|
| **From** | `noreply@unionbank.ng` |
| **Subject** | `UBN BaaS: Verify your email address` |
| **Contents** | A 6-digit OTP code and a link to the verification page |

**OTP** stands for **One-Time Password**: a code that can only be used once. Even if someone else sees the code, they can't use it after you've submitted it. The code expires after **24 hours**, so don't wait too long.

<Tip>
  Check your spam or junk folder if you don't see the email within 5 minutes. Some corporate email servers apply aggressive filtering to automated messages. If your IT team manages your email, ask them to whitelist `noreply@unionbank.ng`.
</Tip>

---

## How to submit your verification code

Verify your email through the portal at [ubn-ui.onecluster.co](https://ubn-ui.onecluster.co):

1. Open the verification email and copy the 6-digit code
2. Go to the verification page in the portal
3. Enter your email address and the 6-digit code
4. Click **Verify Email**

You'll see a confirmation message and your account status will update to `PENDING_KYB_DOCUMENTS`.

---

## What happens next

Your account status is now `PENDING_KYB_DOCUMENTS`. That means:

- You can log in to the portal
- You can see the document upload section
- You can't make authenticated API calls yet. You don't have an API key at this stage. That comes after KYB approval.

Next up: upload your company's compliance documents.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I didn't receive the verification email">
    Try these steps in order:

    1. **Wait 5 minutes.** Email delivery can occasionally take a few minutes, especially through corporate mail servers.
    2. **Check your spam or junk folder.** Search for `noreply@unionbank.ng` or `UBN BaaS`.
    3. **Check your email address.** Log in to the portal and confirm the email address shown in your profile is the one you intended. A typo at registration is a common cause.
    4. **Request a resend.** On the portal login page, click **Resend verification email** and enter your email address.
    5. **Check with your IT team.** Some corporate email systems block automated messages from external senders. Ask your IT team to whitelist `noreply@unionbank.ng`.

    If you've tried all of the above and still have no email after 30 minutes, contact [baas-support@unionbank.ng](mailto:baas-support@unionbank.ng) with your registered email address and the time you registered.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The code says it has expired">
    The OTP code is valid for **24 hours** from the time it was sent. Once it expires, you can't use it.

    To get a new code, go to the portal login page and click **Resend verification email**. A new code will be sent immediately. The old code is invalidated as soon as the new one is generated.

    <Warning>
      Don't re-register if your code has expired. Re-registering with the same email address will fail. Use the resend option instead.
    </Warning>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The code says it has already been used">
    An OTP code can only be used once. If you get a "code already used" error, the code was submitted successfully at some point, possibly by you in a different browser tab, or by a team member who also has access to the email inbox.

    Check your account status:
    - Log in to the portal at [ubn-ui.onecluster.co](https://ubn-ui.onecluster.co)
    - If you see a `PENDING_KYB_DOCUMENTS` status, your email is already verified and you can proceed to Step 3

    If you can't log in and the code is already used, contact [baas-support@unionbank.ng](mailto:baas-support@unionbank.ng).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I entered the code incorrectly too many times">
    After 5 incorrect attempts, the code is locked for security reasons. You'll need to request a new code using the resend option described above.

    If your account is locked, contact [baas-support@unionbank.ng](mailto:baas-support@unionbank.ng) to have the lock cleared manually.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I registered with the wrong email address">
    If you registered with an email address you don't have access to, you can't receive the verification code. In this case:

    1. Contact [baas-support@unionbank.ng](mailto:baas-support@unionbank.ng) with your `partnerId` and the correct email address you intended to use
    2. Our team will update the email on your account and trigger a new verification email

    You can't change your own email address at this stage. It requires manual intervention from our support team.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
